Excellence Has an Origin

Three Generations, Since 1920

Our story begins three generations ago, when Mr. Naseef journeyed from Mesopotamia in 1920, bringing his craft of baking and ice cream-making — including his famous mango-flavored Donderma. As its popularity grew, people simply began calling it Naseef Ice Cream, and it soon grew into Naseef Cafe in the Al-Fadil neighborhood, near the American Mission Hospital.

Naseef Cafe flourished for half a century on that secret recipe before passing to his son, Ibrahim, who kept ice cream at the heart of the business and made the Naseef name synonymous with Eid celebrations across Bahrain. The legacy then passed to his daughter, Amina Naseef, who reopened in the heart of Manama at Bab Al Bahrain in 2013, and went on to open Naseef Al Seef Restaurant in July 2017 — carrying the family's century-old craft into a new generation.

From Our Tanoor

Every loaf at Naseef still comes from a traditional clay tanoor — the same slow, wood-fired method used since 1920. It's the base of our breakfast baskets, our saj, and the fresh bread served with every grilled plate.
