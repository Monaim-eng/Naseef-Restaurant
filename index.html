Our Story

A Muharraq tradition, since 1920

Perched on the Sa'ada Seafront, Naseef Restaurant pairs a century of Bahraini hospitality with a front-row view of the sea, passing yachts, and the Manama skyline. Our bread still comes from an old-fashioned tandoor, baked the way it always has been.

From leisurely breakfasts to family dinners and evening coffee on the terrace, Naseef is built around generous portions, calm surroundings, and food that feels like home — Bahraini classics alongside a few international favorites.
