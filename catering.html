How It Works

Naseef, Delivered to Your Event

Whether it's a family gathering, an office lunch, or a larger celebration, our catering team can put together a spread of Naseef favorites — from breakfast baskets and fresh tanoor bread to mixed grills, biryani, and our famous mango ice cream.

Every order is built around your group size and occasion. Get in touch with your date, headcount, and any preferences, and we'll follow up with options and pricing.

  • Private events & family gatherings
  • Corporate & office catering
  • Custom menus, including vegetarian options
  • Fresh tanoor bread baked to order
  • Delivery available across Muharraq & nearby areas

Note: minimum order sizes and lead times may apply depending on your event — our team will confirm details when you reach out.
